# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: October 4, 2026_

This policy describes the portfolio website in this repository. It is not legal advice. The published site is static and does not currently provide accounts, contact forms, analytics, advertising, or project-managed cookies.

## Information processed when viewing the site

The page loads its portfolio content from the repository's `data.json` file. The site code does not send that content to a project-operated backend or store visitor information in browser storage.

The site currently loads fonts from Google Fonts and the profile image from GitHub's avatar service. A browser may contact those providers to retrieve these resources. Those providers may receive technical information such as an IP address, browser details, and the requested resource; their own privacy policies and practices apply.

The site is hosted using GitHub Pages. GitHub and the infrastructure providers involved in hosting may process technical request data, such as IP addresses and server logs, to deliver and protect the site. See GitHub's privacy statement for details.

## External links and published contact details

The portfolio contains links to third-party websites and contact services. Following a link takes you to that provider, which handles your information under its own policies. The portfolio does not control those services.

Contact details and profile information intentionally included in `data.json` or the page are publicly visible. Contacting the owner by email, phone, or a linked service means that the selected provider will process the information you submit.
