# Code of Conduct

The project maintainer aims to make participation in this project a respectful and welcoming experience for everyone.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate, respectful, and constructive.
- Discuss ideas and criticism in good faith.
- Respect differing viewpoints and experiences.
- Focus feedback on the work, not on the person.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, or personal attacks.
- Deliberate intimidation, insults, or sustained disruption.
- Publishing another person's private information without permission.
- Any other conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional community.

These expectations apply in project issues, pull requests, and other spaces when someone is representing or participating in this project.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report a conduct concern to the maintainer at [alexey.lyapin.inbox@gmail.com](mailto:alexey.lyapin.inbox@gmail.com). Please include what happened and, if relevant, links or other context. The maintainer will review reports and may remove content, close contributions, or restrict participation as appropriate. Reports will be handled as discreetly as practical.
